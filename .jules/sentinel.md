## 2025-05-22 - Loose Access Control and Logic Bypasses in RoomController
**Vulnerability:** Loose `getAccessRules` allowed unauthorized access to several sensitive actions (e.g., `details`, `open`, `create`). Additionally, guests could bypass the 30-minute early join restriction for scheduled streams. Raw exception messages were also leaked in the `actionSchedule` error handling.
**Learning:** Defaulting to permissive access for actions not explicitly listed in `getAccessRules` created a significant attack surface. Logic checks (like the 30-minute rule) that specifically exclude guests (`!isGuest`) can inadvertently grant guests more privileges than authenticated users if not carefully reviewed.
**Prevention:** Always use a "deny by default" or "secure by default" approach in `getAccessRules`. Ensure logic-based access checks (time-based, etc.) include guests in their restrictions unless guests are explicitly meant to have higher privileges. Sanitize all exception messages before displaying them to users.
